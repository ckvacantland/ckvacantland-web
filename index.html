What We Do

CK Vacant Land LLC purchases vacant land directly from property owners in North Carolina, Florida, and Tennessee. We identify landowners through public county tax and property records and reach out to ask if they'd consider selling.

We make cash offers, cover all closing costs, and can close on your timeline — no listing agents, no repairs, no fees.

How It Works

1. We review your property using public records.
2. We reach out by text, phone, or mail with a no-obligation cash offer.
3. If you accept, we handle the paperwork and closing through a licensed title company or attorney, depending on your state.
